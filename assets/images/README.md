## Decorative doodles

`diwali-doodles.svg` is an original, local SVG symbol sprite made for this site. It contains
thin-line motifs for 3D printers, filament spools, extrusion, printed toys, geometric shapes,
stars, sparkles, diyas, rangoli, layer stacks, calipers and a print scraper. It uses no
third-party artwork or remote assets, so there are no external asset licenses or attribution
requirements.

The suggested third-party sources were checked before selection:

- [Open Doodles](https://www.opendoodles.com/) states that its art is CC0; no matching
  illustrations were selected.
- [Icons8 Line illustrations](https://icons8.com/illustrations/styles/line) are free with a
  visible credit link; [Icons8's license](https://icons8.com/license) says websites using free
  graphics should link to Icons8. No matching asset was selected.
- [unDraw Handcrafts](https://handcrafts.undraw.co/) describes its art as free without
  attribution; no matching motif was selected.
- [SVGRepo](https://www.svgrepo.com/) licenses vary by asset and could not be verified for an
  individual selection during review.
- The [Uwarp graphics page](https://www.uwarp.design/tools/icons-and-graphics) could not be
  accessed for license verification.

No third-party artwork was downloaded or included.
